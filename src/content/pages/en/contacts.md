## How we respond

We reply to e-mails sent to `info@tri-s-audit.uz` within two working days. To make our reply specific, please state: the financial reporting framework (NAS or IFRS), the reporting period, the industry and approximate size of the company, and the date by which you need the result.

## Transfer of documents

The website does not accept files. After the first e-mail we agree a secure transfer channel and the list of documents. Personal data of employees and counterparties is de-identified before processing.

## Enquiries and complaints

Questions about the quality of the firm's work, independence or ethics should be sent to the managing partner at the same address, marked "Quality". Every enquiry is logged and handled under the procedure in the firm's Manual.
