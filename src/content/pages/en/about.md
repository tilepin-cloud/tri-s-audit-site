## Who we are

TRI-S-AUDIT is an independent firm: audit, tax and advisory. We have worked in Uzbekistan since 2002 and are entered in the register of audit organisations of the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

We use modern audit methods. Over the years the firm has built a solid client base.

## Main lines of work

- **Audit.** Audit of financial statements prepared under the national standards or IFRS, in accordance with the International Standards on Auditing issued by the International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board (IAASB).
- **Tax advice.** Advice and written opinions on tax.
- **IFRS.** Preparation of IFRS statements, transformation from the national standards, first-time adoption and consolidation.
- **Forensic work.** Financial investigations and the gathering of evidence that can be used in a dispute.

## Other services

- review engagements, agreed-upon procedures and compilation of statements;
- other assurance engagements;
- a report on the security and integrity of accounting system data.

See [Services](/en/services/) for details.

## Independence and quality

We do not audit statements that we prepared or transformed for the same period. The firm's system of quality management follows International Standard on Quality Management 1 issued by the International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board (IAASB). Before issue every report goes through an internal quality review.

## Languages

We write reports, opinions and correspondence in Russian, English and Uzbek.
