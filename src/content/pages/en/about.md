## Who we are

TRI-S-AUDIT is an independent firm: audit, tax and advisory. We have worked in Uzbekistan since 2002 and are entered in the register of audit organisations of the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

We use modern audit methods. Over the years the firm has built a solid client base.

## Main lines of work

- **Audit.** Audit of financial statements prepared under the national standards or IFRS, in accordance with the International Standards on Auditing published by the International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board of the International Federation of Accountants.
- **Tax advice.** Advice on the application of tax legislation and on the preparation of tax calculations and returns.
- **IFRS.** Preparation of financial statements under International Financial Reporting Standards: transformation, first-time adoption and consolidation.
- **Forensic work.** Financial investigations and the gathering of evidence that can be used in a dispute.

## Other services

- reviews of financial statements;
- assurance engagements;
- agreed-upon procedures and compilation of financial information;
- recommendations on the information security of financial information.

See [Services](/en/services/) for details.

## Independence and quality

We do not audit an entity for which, in the audited period, we kept the accounting records or prepared the financial statements, including IFRS statements. Internal quality control of the auditors' work is organised in accordance with the international standards on quality control published by the International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board of the International Federation of Accountants.

## Languages

We write reports, opinions and correspondence in Russian, English and Uzbek.
