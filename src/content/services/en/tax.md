<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> You ask a question and we answer in writing: the conclusion in the first paragraph, then arguments with references to the articles of the Tax Code and other acts in the edition in force on the date of the opinion, an assessment of risks and what to do next. We check every provision on lex.uz before issue.</div>

## Typical questions

- tax consequences of a transaction, reorganisation, dividend payment or loan;
- VAT and profit tax on complex transactions, international transactions and double taxation treaties;
- application of tax incentives and special regimes;
- employment and corporate matters connected with financial reporting;
- support in a tax inspection: preparing objections and a position in a dispute.

## How the opinion is structured

Minto format: the answer, arguments in order of importance, each with a source and edition date, risks with likelihood, impact and mitigation, and open points requiring a client decision. Calculations are independently recomputed before signing.
