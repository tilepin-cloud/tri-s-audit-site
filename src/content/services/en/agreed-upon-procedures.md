<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> Agreed-upon procedures are for when you know exactly what needs to be checked: the intended use of a grant, balances under a contract, a covenant calculation, turnover on a specific account. We perform the list and describe the findings without an opinion and without assurance. Fast, focused, with a clear scope.</div>

## Typical engagements

- intended use of grant or loan funds at the request of a donor or bank;
- confirmation of the calculation of financial covenants;
- testing of individual financial statement items in a transaction;
- reconciliation of balances between group companies;
- testing of the calculation of royalties, bonuses or commissions under a contract.

## How the engagement runs

We agree with you and the user of the report the list of procedures, the data sources and the form of the result. We perform the procedures over the full population of data where possible and describe the factual findings: what was tested, how, and what was found. The report contains no conclusions or recommendations, only facts.
