<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> When a regulator, partner or parent company asks for independent assurance not on the financial statements as a whole but on specific information (an indicator, a procedure, compliance with requirements), ISAE 3000 applies. We agree the subject matter and criteria, perform the procedures and issue a report with reasonable or limited assurance.</div>

## Examples of subject matter

- compliance with the terms of a loan agreement or investment agreement;
- operating indicators disclosed to investors;
- the internal control system of a service organisation (ISAE 3402);
- compliance with regulator requirements for specific procedures.

## How the engagement runs

We agree the subject matter, criteria and intended users, assess whether the subject matter can be measured against the criteria, perform the procedures, evaluate the result and issue the report. The level of assurance and the form of the conclusion are determined by the standard and the contract; the firm does not accept contingent fees for engagements that result in a report.
