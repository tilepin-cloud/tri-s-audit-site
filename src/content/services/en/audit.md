<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> Audit is mandatory for the organisations listed in the Law "On Auditing Activity" (ZRU-677): joint-stock companies, banks, insurers, enterprises with a state share and companies above the established thresholds. The result is an auditor's report accepted by banks, the stock exchange and the Ministry of Finance, and a letter to management with comments on accounting and controls.</div>

## Who needs it

- companies for which audit is mandatory by law;
- groups preparing consolidated IFRS financial statements for investors or a parent company;
- borrowers whose bank requires audited financial statements;
- owners who want an independent assessment of the state of accounting before a transaction.

## How the engagement runs

1. **Acceptance.** Independence and conflict-of-interest check, engagement letter, plan and budget.
2. **Planning.** Understanding of the business, risk assessment, materiality, audit strategy, request for data from the accounting system.
3. **Procedures.** Tests of complete populations of entries, journal entries, confirmations, observation of the stocktake, analytics.
4. **Completion.** Evaluation of misstatements, events after the reporting date, review by the partner responsible for quality, issue of the report.

## Standards and basis

The engagement is performed under the International Standards on Auditing as published in the 2026 IAASB Handbook and recognised in the Republic of Uzbekistan, the Law "On Auditing Activity" and the IESBA Code of Ethics. For small companies the ISA for Audits of Financial Statements of Less Complex Entities (ISA for LCE) is applied where permitted.
