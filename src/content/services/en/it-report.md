<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> Financial statements are only as reliable as the accounting system data behind them. We check who can change entries and how, whether periods are closed, whether register turnovers agree with the financial statements, and whether there are traces of manual intervention, and we issue a report with a clear assessment.</div>

## What we test

- access rights and segregation of duties in the accounting system;
- configuration change management and period closing;
- backup and recovery;
- nine data integrity tests: sequential numbering, reversals, back-dated entries, manual adjustments, equality of turnovers, document-to-entry linkage and others;
- journal entries over the full population with a risk assessment.

## Form of the result

A report for management with an A to D assessment for each area, a list of deficiencies and recommendations. In an audit of financial statements, confirmation of data security and integrity is obtained on every engagement and included in the audit file.
