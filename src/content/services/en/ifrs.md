<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> We turn NAS financial statements into IFRS financial statements that banks and investors accept: with adjustments supported by references to the standards, with notes, and with the reconciliations that IFRS 1 requires on first-time adoption. Every figure is tied to the ledgers by a chain of reconciliations.</div>

## What is included

- analysis of accounting policies and selection of the options permitted by IFRS;
- adjustments: property, plant and equipment and impairment, leases (IFRS 16), financial instruments (IFRS 9), revenue (IFRS 15), deferred taxes (IAS 12), provisions;
- consolidation: scope, intragroup balances and transactions, non-controlling interests;
- notes and disclosures under the current edition of the standards;
- integrity check before release: face-to-note tie-outs, adjustments that sum to zero, and a closed chain from the ledger to the financial statements.

## Form of the result

By default, a set of financial statements with a compilation report under ISRS 4410. If no report is needed, we issue an advisory report without assurance. In both cases you receive the transformation worksheet, which you can update in later periods.
