<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> A review is a quick way to obtain an independent conclusion that the financial statements contain no material misstatements, without a full audit. Procedures: inquiries of management, analytics, agreement to accounting data. Duration: two to four weeks.</div>

## When a review is chosen

- an audit is not mandatory, but a bank or investor asks for assurance on the financial statements;
- interim (quarterly, half-yearly) financial statements of a company whose annual financial statements we audit;
- pre-sale preparation or an assessment of the state of accounting before next year's audit.

## What we do

Inquiries of management about accounting principles and significant transactions, analytical procedures over the full population of accounting data, agreement of the financial statements to the ledgers, evaluation of events after the reporting date. If during the review we find indications of material misstatement, we perform additional procedures or explain why a conclusion cannot be given.
