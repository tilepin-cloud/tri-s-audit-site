<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> When there is a suspicion of theft, misstated financial statements or hidden connections, we reconstruct the picture from accounting system data, bank statements and open sources, record the evidence with a chain of custody and issue a report you can rely on in negotiations, a claim or court.</div>

## When clients come to us

- discrepancies between the financial statements and the actual state of assets;
- suspected related parties and diversion of funds;
- checking a counterparty or partner before a transaction;
- restoring accounting records after a change of management or accountant.

## Principles

The terms of reference are agreed in writing; evidence is gathered lawfully; every fact is corroborated by two independent sources; conclusions are separated from facts; the report is retained for ten years. The firm does not accept engagements in which the client and the subject of the investigation are the same person.
