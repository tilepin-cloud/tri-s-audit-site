<div class="callout"><b>In brief.</b> You have accounting data but no set of financial statements in the required format. We assemble the statements, agree every line to the ledgers and issue a compilation report that tells the user the statements were prepared by specialists but not verified.</div>

## When compilation is needed

- preparing first IFRS financial statements for an investor or bank;
- financial statements of a subsidiary under group standards;
- restoring financial statements for prior periods;
- management accounts in a format close to IFRS.

## How we work

We receive an accounting system extract, build a transformation worksheet, post adjustments with supporting rationale, and assemble the statements and notes. Every figure in the statements is tied to the ledgers by a chain of reconciliations; a break in the chain is a defect that we resolve before issue.
